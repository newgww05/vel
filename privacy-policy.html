Privacy Policy

How Velcura Medical collects, uses and protects the information you share with us.

Effective date: November 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Velcura Medical Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd. (“Velcura Medical”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information collected through velcura.net (the “Website”) and in the course of our business communications. Our Website is intended for business buyers such as distributors, hospital procurement teams and private-label brand partners.

1. Who we are

The party responsible for your personal information is:

Velcura Medical Technology (Shenzhen) Co., Ltd.
Room 2134, 2nd Floor, Building 3, Block 30 (Wenzhengxin Building),
2 Kefa Road, Kejiyuan Community, Yuehai Subdistrict,
Nanshan District, Shenzhen, Guangdong, China
Email: info@velcura.net

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

When you submit an inquiry form, email us or otherwise contact us, we collect the information you choose to provide, such as:

  • your name, company name, job title, email address and phone number;
  • the content of your message, such as products of interest, quantities and target markets;
  • business details you share during our discussions, such as shipping addresses, registration documents or order requirements.

Please do not send us patient information or other health data about individuals. We do not need it to answer business inquiries.

Information collected automatically

  • Inquiry details: the page from which you submitted an inquiry.
  • Usage data: we use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the Website, for example which pages are viewed and whether an inquiry was sent. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar technologies and collects information such as device type, browser, approximate location and pages visited.
  • Security data: our hosting and security provider, Cloudflare, processes technical data such as IP addresses and may set strictly necessary cookies to protect the Website against abuse.

Information from public sources

When we receive an inquiry from a company we have not worked with before, we may review publicly available business information about that company. This can include its website, domain registration records and email domain settings, and checks against international sanctions lists published by government bodies. We do this to verify that we are dealing with a genuine business and to meet our legal obligations.

3. How we use your information

We use personal information to:

  • respond to your inquiries and provide quotations and product information;
  • manage our business relationship with you, including orders, shipping, documentation and after-sales support;
  • keep records of our communications so that our team can serve you consistently;
  • verify business identities, prevent fraud and comply with export control and sanctions laws;
  • improve the Website and understand which content is useful to our visitors;
  • comply with legal, tax and accounting requirements.

Where the law requires a legal basis, we rely on the following: steps you ask us to take before entering into a contract, and the performance of that contract; our legitimate interests in running and protecting our business; compliance with legal obligations; and, where required, your consent (for example, for analytics cookies).

We do not sell personal information. We do not use it for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects on you.

4. Business emails

If we exchange emails, we may follow up on your inquiry or on an ongoing business relationship. Every message is written and sent by a member of our team. If you do not wish to receive further messages from us, reply and tell us, or write to info@velcura.net, and we will stop.

5. How we share information

We share personal information only as needed for the purposes above, with:

  • Service providers who process data on our behalf, including Zoho (business email), Cloudflare (website hosting, security and our internal customer records system) and Google (website analytics);
  • Logistics and trade partners, such as freight forwarders, couriers, customs brokers and banks, when this is necessary to fulfill an order;
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect our rights.

Our service providers may only use the information to provide their services to us.

6. International transfers

We are based in China and serve customers worldwide. Your information may be processed in China and in other countries where we or our service providers operate. These countries may have data protection laws that differ from those in your country. Where required, we take appropriate steps to protect your information when it is transferred.

7. How long we keep information

We keep inquiry and customer records for as long as we have a business relationship with you, and afterwards for as long as needed to handle follow-up questions, resolve disputes and meet legal, tax and accounting requirements. Inquiries that do not lead to a business relationship are reviewed periodically and deleted when they are no longer needed. Analytics data is kept according to our Google Analytics retention settings.

8. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information. These include encrypted connections (HTTPS), access restricted to authorized staff with individual sign-in, activity logging and regular backups. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your information and review our measures over time.

9. Your rights and choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • ask what personal information we hold about you and receive a copy;
  • ask us to correct inaccurate information or delete your information;
  • object to or restrict certain processing, or withdraw consent where we rely on it;
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

To make a request, email info@velcura.net. We may need to confirm your identity before we respond. We will reply within the time required by applicable law.

Cookies: you can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking strictly necessary security cookies may affect how the Website works.

10. Children

The Website is intended for business users and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

11. Third-party links

The Website may link to other websites, for example WhatsApp or social media. Their privacy practices are governed by their own policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

13. Contact us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at info@velcura.net or write to the address in section 1.
